Privacy

Plain and short: what we ask for on this site, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we ask for

When you fill out a form here, we get what you type in: your name, business name, phone and email, and, if you add them, your town, type of business, best time to reach you, what you'd like help with and the numbers you typed in the calculator. We also note which page you came from.

Why

To prepare and send your Online Check, and to follow up on it. That's it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information, and we only use it to answer your request.
  • We don't send you text messages.
  • We don't add you to a mailing list.

Want it deleted?

Email hello@alloverthemoon.com and we'll delete what you sent us.
